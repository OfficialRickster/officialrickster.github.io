About Me & My Work

Photo

I am a System Designer, focused on turning mechanics into balanced, scalable game systems.

  • Logic & Architecture: Collaborating with programmers to define gameplay logic or authoring from scratch.
  • Data Modeling: Structuring formulas and balance tables in spreadsheets to simulate and predict system behaviors.
  • Iterative Tuning: In-engine testing and adjusting values until mechanics feel satisfying and reward player mastery.
  • QA & Diagnostics: Stress-testing edge cases and reporting defects early to keep systems rock-solid.
